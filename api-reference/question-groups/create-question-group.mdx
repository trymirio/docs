---
title: "Create question group"
api: "POST /question-groups"
description: "Create a question group or a page"
---

Creates an empty group, at the end of its parent unless you send `after_position`. Add questions with [Create questions in batch](/api-reference/questions/batch-questions) (`question_group_id`) or [Move questions to a group](/api-reference/question-groups/move-questions).

A page (`kind: "page"`) holds only questions — no subgroups or randomizers — of these types: `multipleChoice`, `ratingScale`, `matrixRatingScale`, `matrixSelection`, `nps`, `starRating`, `ranking`, `rankingWithMatrix`, `allocation`, `allocationWithMatrix`, `number`, `dateInput`, `email`, `shortText`, `textInput`, `message`. Its questions can't show a Figma prototype or an embedded website, or have an AI follow-up (`ai_probe`) turned on. Logic can jump to a page (`move_to_question_group`) but not to a question inside it, and display conditions, checks against earlier answers and `{{Q:...}}` references can't use a source on the same page. See [Several questions on one page](/studies/question-types#several-questions-on-one-page).

<ParamField body="study_id" type="string" required>The study ID.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="title" type="string" required>Internal title; a page shows it as its heading.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="parent_question_group_id" type="string">A top-level group to nest this one in. Omit for the top level. A page can't be a parent.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="is_randomize" type="boolean" default="false">Shuffle the group's direct children for each participant.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="kind" type="string" default="group">`group` or `page`. Any other value returns `422`.</ParamField>
<ParamField body="after_position" type="string" default="end">`end`, `start`, or the ID of a question, group or randomizer directly in the parent; the new group goes right after it.</ParamField>

### Response

Returns the created group (status `201`).

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">The group ID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="study_id" type="string">The study the group belongs to.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="parent_question_group_id" type="string | null">The group it sits in, or `null` at the top level. Groups nest at most two levels.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="title" type="string">Internal title. A page shows it as its heading.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="display_order" type="integer">Position among its siblings (questions and groups share one order).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="is_randomize" type="boolean">Whether the group shuffles its direct children for each participant.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="is_pinned" type="boolean">Whether the group keeps its position when its parent is randomized.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="kind" type="string">`group` (questions asked one at a time) or `page` (the web interviewer shows all of its questions on one screen; voice interviews still ask them one at a time).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="routing_branch_id" type="string | null">The randomizer arm that owns this group, if any.</ResponseField>
