---
title: "Review study"
api: "GET /studies/{id}/review"
description: "Check whether a study is ready to share, and how long it takes"
---

Checks a study before you share its link: whether participants can take it, how long the interview runs, and what could be improved. The checks are deterministic and read-only; nothing is changed.

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The study ID.
</ParamField>

### Response

<ResponseField name="study_id" type="string">The study ID.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="title" type="string">The study title.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="status" type="string">The study status (e.g. `published`, `paused`).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="mode" type="string">The interview mode (e.g. `TEXT_ONLY`, `VIDEO`).</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="ready" type="boolean">`false` while any finding has severity `blocker`.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="question_count" type="integer">Active questions in the study.</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="estimated_minutes" type="object | null">
  `{ min_minutes, max_minutes }`, or `null` when the study has no questions.
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="estimate_basis" type="string | null">
  `longest_path` (the longest route through the study's branching) or `all_questions` (a sum of every question, used when no branching data exists yet).
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="findings" type="array">
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="code" type="string">
      `not_published`, `empty_study`, `prototype_goal_equals_start` (blockers); `empty_prompt`, `high_burden` (warnings); `over_length`, `desktop_only_recommended`, `mergeable_ai_questions` (suggestions).
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="severity" type="string">`blocker`, `warning`, or `suggestion`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="message" type="string">What was found.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="suggestion" type="string">The suggested fix.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="question_ids" type="string[]">The questions involved, when the finding is about specific questions.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
<ResponseField name="judgment_checks" type="string[]">
  Wording and design checks that need someone reading the questions (leading or double-barreled wording, order, sample size).
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl https://api.getversive.com/api/v1/studies/9b2f7c1e-4a4b-4f6e-9a1d-1c2d3e4f5a6b/review \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $VERSIVE_API_KEY"
```
</RequestExample>
