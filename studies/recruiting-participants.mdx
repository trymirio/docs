There are three ways to get participants into a study:

1. **Bring your own** — share the [study link](/studies/launch-and-sharing#link) or [embed the study](/sdk/overview) in your product.
2. **Your audience** — invite contacts you've saved in Versive via [email outreach](/studies/launch-and-sharing#send-email-outreach).
3. **The participant panel** — recruit vetted participants through Versive's Prolific or Respondent integrations.

## Your audience

The **Audience** is your organization's contact list — a lightweight research CRM.

- Import contacts in bulk (deduplicated by email) or add them individually, with arbitrary metadata fields that become filterable columns.
- Add contacts to any study in one step: each gets a personal tracked interview link.
- Contact statuses track deliverability and consent — **unsubscribed** and **bounced** contacts are automatically excluded from future sends.

## The participant panel

The **Recruit** tab connects your study to consumers and professionals through two panel providers:

- **Prolific** is suited to fast consumer and general-population studies, with a searchable catalog of built-in audience filters.
- **Respondent** is suited to B2B and harder-to-reach professional audiences, with custom targeting criteria and screener questions.

### Setting up a Respondent project

1. Define your **target audience** — B2C or B2B targeting with demographics (age, education, household income, and more) and professional attributes (industries, skills, seniority, company size).
2. Add **screener questions** — multiple choice, checkboxes, text, numeric, or slider-scale questions that qualify participants before they reach your study.
3. Set the **incentive** — as a rule of thumb, budget about $1 per minute of study length; B2B audiences typically require more.

### Setting up a Prolific project

1. Set the **participant count, estimated duration, and reward**. Versive displays the estimated total, including provider fees, before checkout.
2. If your study uses screen-out logic, Versive turns on screen-out payments automatically: set the **max screen-outs** and the **screen-out reward** (see below).
3. Search Prolific's filter catalog and add the demographic or behavioral criteria you need. You can assign distribution quotas across selected filter values.
4. Review the audience and cost, then choose **Pay and publish**. You can pause or resume recruitment from the project afterward.

Versive creates the required Prolific completion codes and sends participants back to Prolific automatically. Prolific projects cannot use Versive **redirect** logic, since the integration owns where participants go when they finish.

#### Screening participants out

Prolific's audience filters exclude people before they start your study, at no cost. Screen-out logic on a question inside your survey is different: those participants have already started, so Prolific requires them to be paid. When your study has screen-out logic, the Prolific project's **Screen-out questions** toggle is on and locked, and the section shows which questions screen participants out. A study without screen-out logic needs no screen-out budget: everyone who starts it counts as a completion. (If you plan to add screen-out rules after launch, turn the toggle on anyway; a budget can't be added once the study is published.)

- **Max screen-outs** — the number of paid screen-outs you're budgeting for. It defaults to one per participant, and Versive shows the qualify rate your budget implies (for example, 20 screen-outs for 20 participants assumes about 50% of people who start the study qualify). Screened-out participants don't use up study places. When the limit is reached, Prolific pauses recruiting and Versive emails your workspace owners, admins and creators.
- **Screen-out reward** — a fixed amount paid automatically to each screened-out participant. Prolific requires at least $0.14 and less than the participant reward. The default is $0.25; set it by how long participants spend before the screen-out question.

The screen-out budget (reward × max screen-outs, plus Prolific fees) is included in the checkout total; unused screen-out budget is refunded after you close the project (Versive processes the refund). The reward can't be changed after publishing, but while the study is active or paused you can **add screen-out slots** from the project's Submissions tab — pay for the extra slots and recruiting resumes automatically if Prolific had paused it. Unused top-up budget is included in the refund when you close the project.

Screened-out participants appear as **Screened out** in the project's submissions, and each panel row shows screen-outs used against the limit.

### Drafting a panel with the assistant

You can also ask the Versive Assistant (⌘J) to set up a panel: for example, "Recruit 50 US adults on Prolific for this study, $8 for 10 minutes." It reads the study, looks up the provider's targeting options, shows you the plan with a cost estimate, and asks you to confirm before it creates anything. The result is a **draft** panel on the Recruit tab with a link to the review page; the assistant never pays or publishes, so nothing is charged until you choose **Pay and publish** there. If the study screens participants out, a Prolific draft includes the screen-out reward and the number of paid screen-outs, and the estimate covers them; buying more screen-out slots later is done from the panel. You can ask it to adjust a draft (participants, reward, filters, screen-outs, screener questions) or to check whether a draft is ready to publish. The same tools are available to agents connected through the [MCP server](/mcp/tools).

### Tracking recruitment

Panel projects have a lifecycle of **Draft → Active → Closed / Archived** and can be paused, resumed, duplicated, or closed. Respondent projects show funnel counts such as requested, invited, survey taken, qualified, and paid. Prolific projects show each submission's live status (including screened-out participants and how many screen-out slots remain) and let you approve, reject, or bulk-approve responses awaiting review.

Closing a Respondent project approves completed respondents for payment. You can also export Respondent profiles (name, job title, company, LinkedIn, skills, location, match quality) to CSV.

<Callout icon="info-circle" color="#71717a">
Panel recruiting is billed pay-as-you-go on top of your subscription — you pay per qualified participant, not from your credit allowance.
</Callout>

## Which approach when?

| Situation | Best option |
| --- | --- |
| You have engaged users and want product feedback | Your own link or the Embed SDK |
| You have a list of customers/leads to interview | Audience + email outreach |
| You need specific demographics or professions you don't have access to | Participant panel |
| You want feedback *today* and can't wait for recruiting | [AI tests](/ai-tests/overview) or [simulated interviews](/studies/launch-and-sharing#simulate-ai-test-participants) |
