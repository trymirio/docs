A **study** is Versive's core research unit: a set of questions that participants complete through a shareable link, an embed in your product, or a recruited panel. Depending on how you configure it, a study can feel like a traditional survey, a conversational AI-moderated interview, or a full voice/video interview.

## Study types

Every study has one of two types, chosen when you create it and changeable later in **Settings → General**:

| Study type | What it does | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI interview** | AI-moderated interview: participants answer, the AI asks follow-ups | 1 AI credit per completion (2 in the Voice to voice Realtime conversation mode) |
| **Survey** | Classic survey with no AI questions — structured question types only | 1 survey response per completion, no AI credits |

AI interviews need a plan with AI credits. On the Free plan, new studies are surveys, and a survey can't be switched to an AI interview until you upgrade. See [Plans and usage](/getting-started/concepts#plans-and-usage).

## Conversation modes

The conversation mode controls how participants respond. Surveys support Text only and Text and voice; AI interviews can use any mode.

| Mode | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Text only** | Participants type their answers in a chat interface |
| **Text and voice** | Participants can type or speak (default) |
| **Voice only** | Participants answer by voice |
| **Voice with preview** | Participants speak, then review and edit the transcription before submitting |
| **Video** | Records participant video alongside the interview |
| **Voice to voice Realtime** | A live spoken conversation with the AI on the realtime engine, with a configurable silence threshold (2 AI credits per completion) |

## Ways to create a study

- **AI study assistant** — describe your research goals in natural language and the assistant generates the full study: questions, settings, and logic. Keep chatting to refine it — add, reorder, or rewrite questions, change settings, and update logic rules, with structured change tracking. You can also import a Figma prototype and get auto-suggested questions.
- **Manual creation** — build question by question with the drag-and-drop builder and the question library.
- **Templates** — start from pre-built or organization-specific [study templates](/platform/templates).

## Study status

New studies are live by default — they accept participants as soon as you share the link. You can **pause** a study (visitors are sent to your custom pause URL if one is set) and **archive** it when the research is done. A study also stops accepting new participants once it reaches its [quota](/studies/settings#quotas); the study itself stays live so anyone mid-interview can finish.
