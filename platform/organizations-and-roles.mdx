An **organization** is your team's workspace — it owns all studies, AI tests, personas, templates, billing, and API keys. Users can belong to multiple organizations and switch between them, which is how agencies keep client work separated.

## Inviting members

Owners and admins can invite teammates by email from the organization settings. Members sign in with email/password or Google, and can secure their account with **multi-factor authentication** (TOTP authenticator apps). The number of seats is set by your plan.

If Google sign-in fails, Versive shows a general sign-in error. Retry signing in; if the problem continues, contact support.

## Organization roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and subscription management — the only role that can change the plan. |
| **Admin** | Manage everything except billing (can view it): members, org settings, studies, folders, and the Library. |
| **Creator** | Create and manage studies, folders, and Library content. No access to org settings, members, or billing. |
| **Member** | View studies and folders — read-only. |

Only owners and admins can add, change, or remove members.

## Organization settings

Organization settings are split across tabs:

- **Team** — your organization name, the logo shown in your workspace, and members.
- **Plans** — your subscription and billing.
- **Settings** — study branding defaults and embed domains, covered below.
- **API** — organization-scoped keys for the [public API](/api-reference/introduction), available to enterprise organizations.

### Study branding defaults

Every study carries its own branding — the logo and color participants see. Rather than restyle each new study by hand, owners and admins can set the branding new studies start from under **Organization > Settings > Study defaults**:

| Default | Applies to |
| --- | --- |
| **Study logo** | The logo in the header participants see. New studies with no logo of their own get this one. |
| **Primary color** | The accent color of the participant experience. Must be a 6-digit hex value like `#00786F`. |

Two things are worth knowing about how these are applied:

- **They are copied in at creation, not linked.** A study takes a snapshot of the defaults the moment it is created and owns its branding from then on, so changing a default later leaves existing studies alone. To restyle a study that already exists, edit its [Branding](/studies/settings#branding) tab.
- **An explicit choice always wins.** Only branding the creator left unset is filled in from the defaults. This holds however the study is created — the dashboard, the [API](/api-reference/studies/create-study), or the AI agent. Studies duplicated or created from a template keep the source's branding and are not touched at all.

Each study can still change or remove its branding in its own [Branding](/studies/settings#branding) tab.

### Embed domains

The domain allow-list required before a study can be embedded with the [Embed SDK](/sdk/security). Studies cannot be embedded anywhere until at least one domain is added.

## Two permission layers

Organization roles set what someone can do *globally*. Access to specific folders and studies is refined by a second layer — [folder & study roles](/platform/folders-and-permissions). A user's effective access to a study is the combination of both.
