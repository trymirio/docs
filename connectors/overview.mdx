Connectors link your Versive organization to other tools, so study activity reaches your team where they already work. You connect a tool, then choose on each study how to use it.

## Manage connectors

Connectors live in **Organization → Connectors**. Organization-wide connectors (Slack, Webhooks) are connected once for the whole organization by owners and admins; see [Organizations & roles](/platform/organizations-and-roles). Google Sheets is a personal connection: any member connects their own Google account, and only they can disconnect it.

Select a connector to open its panel. Each connected tool shows the account it's connected to and its status:

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Versive can use the tool. |
| **Reconnect needed** | The tool stopped accepting Versive's access, for example because someone removed the app. Open the connector and reauthorize it. |

Before you disconnect a tool, its panel lists the studies that use it. Disconnecting removes Versive's access to the tool. Studies keep their settings and pick up again when you reconnect the same account.

## Security

Versive asks each tool for the smallest set of permissions it needs, and stores access tokens encrypted. Disconnecting deletes them.
