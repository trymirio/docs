Versive is an AI-powered user research and testing platform. It combines two complementary product lines on a single platform, with shared analysis, personas, and team workspaces:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Studies" icon="messages" href="/studies/overview">
    AI-moderated surveys and interviews with real participants. Text, voice, and video modes, 18+ question types, conditional logic, and translations into 20 languages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI Tests" icon="robot" href="/ai-tests/overview">
    Instant usability tests using AI personas on Figma prototypes, live websites, and uploaded designs. Reports with transcripts, screenshots, and heuristic evaluations in minutes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Both product lines share a unified analysis layer with AI-powered insights, theme extraction, sentiment analysis, and exportable reports.
